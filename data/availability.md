## Notes

- **Interactive Brokers**: US-listed ETFs generally have no KID, so EEA/UK retail clients may not buy them. Listed ways around it: buy the EU-issued equivalent ETF (has a KID), trade a CFD on a large US ETF, or ask to be reclassified from Retail to Professional.
- **Degiro**: no DEGIRO-owned page found; only third-party reviews and Trustpilot replies.
- **Fio**: says the Czech National Bank took a lenient position on PRIIPs, so Fio clients can still trade US ETFs without restriction. Fio cannot advise on them or actively offer them. Other brokers stopped offering US ETFs. A separate Fio ETF list page says Fio does not offer ETFs itself, it only gives access to markets where some can be traded. The full 31-page ETF list was not checked.
- **XTB**: nothing verified for US-listed ETFs. Found only XTB Limited's KID for CFDs on ETFs (a derivative, not the fund itself) and XTB product pages for UCITS ETFs, with no statement about US-listed ETFs.
- **Portu**: no portu.cz page found; only third-party articles.
